One image, one day: Wednesday 25 November 2026, laid out as a 24-hour
timeline. Six stories stand as blocks on a 00:00–24:00 UTC axis, and the
taller the block, the heavier the story weighed on the day.

The day opens just eighteen minutes in, at 00:18 UTC, with Shenzhou-24
lifting off for Tiangong carrying Pakistan's first astronaut. In Tehran,
two dawn executions of 2022 protesters are halted hours before the
hanging. In West Java, rescuers work a flooded gold mine at Pongkor
where 9 miners are dead and 17 remain trapped. In Dhaka, garment workers
win a 36% pay rise to $139 a month after weeks of protests. In Iowa, the
Army's controlled detonation of 9,500 lbs of ageing explosive goes off
without a hitch. And towering over them all, the tallest block of the
day: the USTR tariff findings landing on 14 economies and rattling
markets across Asia.

The footer carries the day's closing numbers: S&P 500 7,182.78 (−0.25%),
Nasdaq 23,585.80 (−0.62%), Dow 50,182.95 (+0.33%), Nikkei 57,011.62
(−2.78%), Brent crude around $94.61 (+3.51%), Nvidia $149.30 (−3.91%),
and US core PCE at +3.3% year on year.

Sources: AP · Reuters · USTR · BEA · USA Today.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

That writes `entry.png` into `out/`. The entrypoint is plain Python 3
standard library — no extra packages, no network — it simply places the
finished image at the output path.
